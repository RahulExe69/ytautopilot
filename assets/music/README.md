# Optional background music

Drop one short music track here as `background.mp3`, `background.wav`, `background.m4a`, `background.aac`, or `background.ogg`.

The Stage 3 renderer automatically picks the first supported track and mixes it very quietly underneath narration and gameplay audio.

Only use music that you own or are licensed/authorized to reuse. Do not upload copyrighted songs just because they are available elsewhere online.
